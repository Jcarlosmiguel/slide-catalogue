Add Slides to Virtual Learning Materials

The final step - embedding the slide into a course in your institution's VLE (Moodle, Canvas, Blackboard, or similar). This page describes the general pattern; the exact steps depend on which VLE integration your institution has set up.

The general pattern

A slide embedded in a VLE typically works through a gated proxy, not a direct link to OMERO:

  • The VLE integration is configured with one or more subject/course accounts in OMERO - the same course-specific accounts set up in the previous step (see Prepare the Slide for View on the VLE).
  • A member of course staff embeds a slide by its OMERO Image ID into a page, label, or similar content item in the VLE, picking the subject account that matches the course.
  • When a student views that page, the VLE checks they're enrolled on the course, then serves the slide through a proxy using the shared subject account - the student never needs (or gets) their own OMERO login or credentials, and the real OMERO server address never appears in what they see.

Some integrations also support annotating specific views of the slide with linked commentary text, or setting which part of the slide the embed opens on by default - check your specific integration's own documentation for what it supports.

If your institution doesn't have a VLE integration like this yet, the underlying pattern (an enrolment-gated reverse proxy in front of OMERO, using a shared per-course service account) is a reasonable starting point for building one.
